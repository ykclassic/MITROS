# Phase 6 — Regime + Statistical Intelligence

Phase 6 introduces deterministic descriptive market intelligence.

## Regime detection
Classifies verified candle history into trend-up, trend-down, range, high-volatility, low-volatility, or unknown using configurable return and volatility thresholds.

## Statistical intelligence
Computes mean return, volatility, win rate, downside deviation, lag-1 autocorrelation, and standardized mean-return z-score.

## Safety boundary
These outputs are analytical evidence only. They do not authorize trades, override risk controls, or execute orders.

## Integrity
All calculations require verified, chronologically ordered candles from one asset, venue, and timeframe. Decimal arithmetic is used throughout.
