# Phase 14 — Research Platform

Phase 14 turns the Phase 9 research primitives into a coherent, provenance-first research platform.

## Capabilities

- immutable research queries and reports
- dataset/backtest/walk-forward/replay artifact registration
- artifact checksums and metadata for reproducibility
- deterministic metric comparison between research reports
- bounded research windows
- no live execution dependency

## Boundary

Research remains downstream of market data and strategy/intelligence evidence. It cannot modify risk limits, human approval state, execution ledger state, or venue state.

The platform is the structured substrate for future research UI and AI research assistance without turning the AI layer into an execution authority.
